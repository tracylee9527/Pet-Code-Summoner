Dimensions

Beat every stage in a dimension to open the next one!
Type advantage: Volcanic (Fire) pets deal 1.25x to Frozen (Ice) enemies. Void (Galaxy) pets deal 1.5x to both Volcanic and Frozen enemies. Overworld has no type either way.
♾️ Infinity Depths (unlocked after The Void) never ends - clearing a level generates the next one. A new catchable pet unlocks every 20 levels, and the mythical ★★★★★ King Dragon appears on every 100th level. Starting at level 150 and every 20 levels after (150, 170, 190...), earn 2 Doughnuts, each an instant level 200 for a pet of your choice!
🎁 Every 5th level cleared in a regular dimension (Overworld, Volcanic Depths, Frozen Abyss, The Void - not Infinity Depths) drops a treasure chest with 2 accessories - Hats, Socks, Armor, and Necklaces, each in 3 styles and 5 rarities (Common +1/+1 up to Mythic +5/+5 HP/Attack). Higher dimensions unlock rarer gear: Overworld (common/uncommon/rare), Volcanic Depths & Frozen Abyss (uncommon/rare/epic), The Void (rare/epic/mythic). Equip gear from any pet's stats popup to see it on your pet, or merge two of the same type + style + rarity in 🎒 Accessories for the next tier up.
🏋️ Training Grounds drops its own treasure chest every 3rd round cleared - steeper odds than anywhere else: 40% Rare, 59% Epic, and 1% Mythic, never Common or Uncommon. Every round's boss is always exactly 3x as strong as an Infinity Depths enemy at your CURRENT Infinity Depths level - it tracks your real Infinity Depths progress, not how many Training Grounds rounds you've cleared.
